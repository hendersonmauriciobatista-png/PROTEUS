# ICFACTORY Document Map

This document explains where to find the main ICFACTORY documents and when to read them.

It is a navigation map, not a tutorial and not a glossary.

## How To Use This Map

Use this map when you know what you are trying to do but do not know which document to open.

For first contact, do not begin with the full map. Read the root `README.md` and `GETTING_STARTED.md` first.

## Recommended First-Contact Path

1. `README.md`
2. `GETTING_STARTED.md`
3. `CONSTITUTION.md`
4. `governance/GOVERNANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`
5. `governance/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION_TEMPLATE.md`
6. `DOCUMENT_MAP.md`

This path gives orientation before references.

## Entry Documents

`README.md`

Purpose: first orientation for external readers.

Read when: you are opening the repository for the first time.

Do not use it for: full constitutional definitions or historical decisions.

`GETTING_STARTED.md`

Purpose: first adoption path.

Read when: you want to begin using ICFACTORY in a project.

Do not use it for: complete semantic definitions or document history.

`DOCUMENT_MAP.md`

Purpose: navigation across the document set.

Read when: you need to know which artifact serves which purpose.

Do not use it for: step-by-step adoption or concept definitions.

## Normative Documents

`CONSTITUTION.md`

Purpose: defines foundational principles for ICFACTORY.

Read when: you need to understand the non-negotiable constitutional direction of the framework.

`CONSTITUTIONAL_LEXICON.md`

Purpose: authoritative semantic reference for constitutional terms.

Read when: you need precise meaning for a consolidated constitutional concept.

Do not read it first unless your task is semantic audit.

`governance/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION_TEMPLATE.md`

Purpose: template for creating a Project Constitution under ICFACTORY.

Read when: you are ready to start adoption in a project.

Do not use it as a general explanation of the framework.

## Governance Documents

`governance/GOVERNANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`

Purpose: explains the governance hierarchy and how authority flows.

Read when: you need to understand the relationship between the ICFACTORY Constitution, a Project Constitution, operational governance and the system.

`governance/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION_ALFA_DRAFT.md`

Purpose: draft example of a Project Constitution.

Read when: you need an example of template usage and understand that it is not approved for operational use.

Do not treat it as an authoritative constitution for your project.

## Concept Documents

`concepts/README.md`

Purpose: explains how official ICFACTORY concepts are structured.

Read when: you need to understand how concept documents are organized.

`concepts/ACI.md`

Purpose: explains controlled institutional audit.

Read when: your task involves diagnosing before changing.

`concepts/AUDIT_PLAYBOOK.md`

Purpose: explains the operational protocol for structured audits.

Read when: you need to conduct or review an audit process.

`concepts/CIEX.md`

Purpose: explains contextual structural analysis for exceptions and tensions.

Read when: a rule, context and runtime behavior appear to conflict.

`concepts/OSE.md`

Purpose: explains structural observability.

Read when: logs, states, symptoms or operational visibility are central to the issue.

`concepts/ALO.md`

Purpose: explains the adaptive operational governance layer.

Read when: you need to understand how operational guidance relates to constitutional boundaries.

## Historical And Planning Documents

`HISTORY.md`

Purpose: institutional record of decisions, audits and changes.

Read when: you need provenance, prior decisions or evolution context.

Do not use it as the first onboarding document.

`ROADMAP.md`

Purpose: planning and status record for ICFACTORY programs and layers.

Read when: you need to understand program status, completed layers or future work.

Do not use it as the primary adoption guide.

## Research Documents

`research/DISCOVERY_LIFECYCLE.md`

Purpose: official process for recording, validating, reviewing, promoting and consolidating Discoveries.

Read when: you need to understand how Research hypotheses move through the Discovery Lifecycle.

GP-R01 status: validated as the official lifecycle for the Research layer.

`research/methodology/METHODOLOGICAL_DISCOVERIES.md`

Purpose: inventory of Methodological Discoveries.

Read when: you need to review DM candidates such as DM-01 - Identificacao Do Nucleo Do Negocio.

GP-R01 status: DM-01 remains CANDIDATA.

`research/architecture/ARCHITECTURAL_DISCOVERIES.md`

Purpose: inventory of Architectural Discoveries.

Read when: you need to review DA candidates such as DA-01 - Especializacao Por Contexto Operacional and DA-02 - Auditoria De Integracao Arquitetural.

GP-R01 status: DA-01 and DA-02 remain CANDIDATA.

Research boundary:

GP-R is the program line for Research evolution. GP-R programs do not promote Discoveries automatically to the official ICFACTORY core.

## Read According To Your Goal

To understand what ICFACTORY is:

1. `README.md`
2. `CONSTITUTION.md`
3. `governance/GOVERNANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`

To begin adoption:

1. `GETTING_STARTED.md`
2. `governance/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION_TEMPLATE.md`
3. `CONSTITUTIONAL_LEXICON.md` as needed

To conduct an audit:

1. `concepts/ACI.md`
2. `concepts/AUDIT_PLAYBOOK.md`
3. `concepts/CIEX.md` when context tension matters

To understand terminology:

1. `CONSTITUTIONAL_LEXICON.md`
2. `LEXICON.md` when working with earlier or broader terminology

To understand institutional evolution:

1. `HISTORY.md`
2. `ROADMAP.md`

## Boundary Reminder

ICFACTORY is the framework.

H&A is an implementation context present in this repository.

When adopting ICFACTORY elsewhere, use the constitutional, governance, concept and onboarding documents as the framework source. Treat H&A runtime files as implementation-specific context unless your adoption target is H&A itself.
